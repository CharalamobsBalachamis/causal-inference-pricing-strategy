# Causal Impact Analysis: Marketing ROI via Difference-in-Differences

### The Business Problem
When a business launches a new marketing campaign or pricing strategy, sales usually go up. But how much of that growth was actually caused by the campaign, and how much was just natural seasonality or macroeconomic trends? 

Naive "before-and-after" comparisons routinely overestimate marketing effectiveness by failing to account for these underlying trends, leading executives to misallocate future budgets.

### The Solution
This project replaces simple historical comparisons with a rigorous **Causal Inference** approach. Using a **Difference-in-Differences (DiD)** econometric framework, this pipeline isolates the *true* incremental revenue generated by a localized intervention. 

By comparing a treatment region (São Paulo) against highly correlated control regions (Rio de Janeiro, Minas Gerais), the model filters out time-based shocks and baseline regional differences to reveal the exact financial impact of the management decision.

### Key Features & Logic
* **Causal Impact Estimator:** Implements a DiD model to isolate the net effect of the intervention from background market noise.
* **Trend & Seasonality Control:** Utilizes a Two-Way Fixed Effects (TWFE) framework via the `fixest` package to ensure robustness under the Parallel Trends assumption.
* **ROI Decomposition:** Quantifies the exact gap between naive revenue attribution (guessing) and true causal impact (mathematical proof), highlighting how traditional methods mislead decision-making.

### Business Impact (The Results)
* **True Uplift:** Identified a statistically significant ($p < 0.05$) true incremental revenue increase of **~104,500 BRL per month**.
* **Prevented Misattribution:** Demonstrated that a standard naive analysis would have falsely attributed an extra ~82,000 BRL/month to the campaign, inflating the perceived ROI.
* **Total Value:** Proved a total incremental revenue generation of **~836,400 BRL** over the 8-month post-intervention period.

### Tech Stack
* **Language:** R
* **Libraries:** `tidyverse` (Data Wrangling), `fixest` (High-dimensional Fixed Effects), `lubridate` (Time-series formatting), `ggplot2` (Data Visualization).
